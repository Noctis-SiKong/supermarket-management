## 2 Project Overview and Boundaries

A single physical supermarket, PC-based, with administrator, store manager and sales assistant as users. P1 is a lightweight local Java CUI, single-machine single-process, with a core that works offline; first-time acquisition of build dependencies and offline business operation are verified separately. The goal is to connect actual-arrival review, in-warehouse/on-shelf transfer, sales and original actual paid, promotions, returns and exchanges/loss, and traceability, while satisfying the course's requirements/design/HAI evidence requirements.

The agreed P1 scope is in FR-01–15: barcode products, receipt pending review/approved into warehouse, CUI import with optional images, two-location ledger entries/opening balances, on-shelf sales and payment confirmation, whole-document single-activity best-option selection, 30-day returns and exchanges/partial returns/quantity redemption/difference, recording permissions, voiding reverse linkage, JSON file recovery, replenishment and loss-registration handling. Product date and batch capabilities are all withdrawn per U6.

Explicitly excluded: chain supermarkets, online stores, weighing/fractional quantities, prior approval of purchase requests or orders, a separate reviewer/mandatory two-person review, store-manager approval for each daily return or exchange, a third unsellable stock bucket, a real payment gateway, barcode scanning that depends on dedicated hardware, and no held orders. Supplier purchase returns are not included, and corrections must not be confused with returns to supplier. Interface boundaries for future barcode scanning/hardware integration are retained, without connecting hardware or claiming implementation; no P2 implementation is done for reservations. Financial accounting and tax, payroll, points and other features that were not originally included are still not added.

**Business judgement and system boundaries**: This system is for daily supermarket goods management, not an ERP or monetary-audit system. Roles represent system permissions and do not restrict the actual number of people; multiple store managers are allowed, and a single store manager is also allowed; formal separation of duties must not be used to require that the purchaser and the reviewer be different people, and a store manager may purchase and self-review under the existing rules without an additional store manager or second approver. Store managers and sales assistants exercise business judgement under the existing permissions and rules, and the system must not add per-transaction approval, two-person check or other management chains on its own. Return/exchange eligibility, quantity limits, monetary handling and stock impact follow the existing confirmed rules; no case-by-case store-manager eligibility authorization exception is provided. The reason option "quality/other" is for recording only, and other reasons must be filled in as text; this does not change the 30-day, opened/quality or quantity/amount/stock validations. Data-correctness constraints such as non-negative stock, returned plus exchanged not exceeding the originally purchased quantity, after-sales amount quota, prevention of duplicate refunds and business records remain in effect, and product amount calculation and the transaction records required for after-sales must not be removed. Except where the existing approved baseline explicitly requires it, no ERP, financial audit, monetary audit, business approval or independent audit reporting scope is added; existing business traceability is not cancelled because of this. For authorization and impact see §17 CR07-08/10.

Per the course, P1 uses Java, a standard Maven or Gradle project, Java collections and File I/O; per the user's choices this project uses Maven, JDK17, JUnit4.13.2, Jackson/UTF-8 JSON, BigDecimal and Asia/Shanghai. Only P2 actually implements the Java GUI and Derby Embedded; the related hard requirements remain in §14. Expired-product sales interception has been cancelled by U6, and no expired-sales rules are inferred.

## 5 User Roles and Permissions

The formal roles are uniformly Administrator, Store Manager, and Clerk. "Back office" always means the role-restricted CUI, not a web page. Every record is associated with a specific operator, and the string "clerk" is not used in place of an individual identity. The Administrator enters the system as the Administrator and is responsible for maintenance/repair; their business review permission is technically allowed, but in principle the Store Manager is responsible for procurement/receipt review — this is an operating policy, not a technical prohibition (U3 Q2/Q4).

| Operation | Administrator | Store Manager | Clerk | Decision basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| System maintenance and repair | Responsible | Not separately authorized | Not separately authorized | Q02 |
| Procurement/actual receipt registration | Not separately authorized for routine registration | May be responsible for procurement and registration | Not authorized in this round | Q04 |
| Receipt review | Technically allowed; in principle does not serve as routine reviewer | Responsible; may self-review | No confirmed review authorization | Q02/Q04 |
| Routine return/exchange submission confirmation | Operator permission not separately extended | May query/audit; no eligibility exceptions, no per-transaction approval | May handle directly, with no Store Manager per-transaction approval | Q08/Q09; U1; CR07-10 |

| Voiding authorization | May authorize | May authorize | Requires Store Manager or Administrator authorization | Q10 |

Administrator/Store Manager/Clerk accounts are preconfigured locally, with username + password and passwords stored as hashes; there is no network authentication. Product maintenance/promotion configuration: Store Manager, with the Administrator able to maintain and correct; sales/replenishment: Clerk and Store Manager; loss registration: Store Manager registers, Administrator corrects; queries: Store Manager/Administrator all, Clerk own; the Administrator may technically review receipts but in principle does not serve as routine reviewer. Other existing authorizations are not expanded.

Receipt entry continues to follow the Store Manager's confirmed responsibility for procurement actual-receipt registration; in this round Clerks are not separately authorized to maintain receipt records, must not operate by default, and an additional entry-permission gap is no longer used to block closure.


After-sales-specific read exception (sources see §17 SYNC-02 and CR07-11): a Clerk's ordinary sales history remains own-only, and the Store Manager/Administrator can still query all. When handling after-sales, the dedicated use of the customer's original sales voucher permits precise location of another person's original sale, returning only the fields necessary for handling, without opening a cross-person list. Only after the IF-11 trace is reliably saved through IF-05 is the minimum original sale data required for handling after-sales returned; if saving fails, nothing is returned for the time being. Cancellation produces no refund, stock or quota change, and completed after-sales does not use this cancellation flow. Approval status is found only in §17; A provides the solution/read trace, B is responsible for the after-sales draft and cancellation orchestration, with centralized audit after delivery.

## 7 Core Business Flows, Quantities and Money

### 7.1 Goods Receipt, Replenishment and Position Effects

Original option A (U3 Q4) has been selected; original option B is no longer a current option. Receipt statuses are pending review, review approved, returned for supplementation/correction; actual-receipt pending-review records are displayed independently, are not counted in either position, and are not a third stock bucket. A business approved by the Store Manager posts once to in-warehouse; returned for correction is not posted, and after correction it is approved and posts only once. Store Manager self-review is allowed; the Administrator may technically review, but in principle the Store Manager handles it. Attachments are imported from a local CUI path and may be skipped for a single transaction; missing images do not automatically reject review. Review correction is not a supplier return; the latter is not within P1 flows.

On-shelf S = shelf, in-warehouse W = warehouse, both non-negative integers, T=S+W; a position is not equivalent to an enabled/sellable status. Balances are an aggregation of effective ledger entries, and caches may be rebuilt; the opening period must have opening inbound ledger entries, and balances cannot be changed directly without a source.

| Action | On-shelf change | In-warehouse change | Ledger entry/result |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Actual receipt registration/pending review/returned for correction | 0 | 0 | Actual receipt and review records retained, quantity listed separately, cannot replenish/sell |
| Review approved | 0 | +q | One inbound ledger entry; repeated approval does not post again |
| In-warehouse staging without movement | 0 | 0 | No duplicate inbound posting |
| Replenishment to shelf | +q | −q | Both-way ledger entries in the same event, T unchanged, blocked if W insufficient |
| Sale confirmation | −q | 0 | From on-shelf only, whole-order payment/outbound/snapshot take effect together, rejected if source insufficient |
| Return/all exchange-returned goods | 0 | 0 | Original sale association, loss/disposition record; neither re-enters stock nor deducts existing stock again |
| Exchange replacement item | −q | 0 | Rejected if on-shelf insufficient; in-warehouse stock cannot be offset directly |
| In-store existing goods loss registration | −q if the source is on-shelf | −q if the source is in-warehouse | Deducted once from the specified source, the other position unchanged, subsequent disposition does not deduct again |
| Authorized voiding | Reverse quantity corresponding to the original business | Reverse quantity corresponding to the original business | Generates reverse ledger entries based on the original document and retains the reason; return/exchange refunds are also reversed synchronously, not merely status-changed; negative stock is not allowed |
| Query/actual disposition of already-recorded loss | 0 | 0 | No automatic adjustment or delisting, no unsellable bucket |

q is an integer business quantity from 1–9999; 0 and out-of-range are rejected and not written to the database (R-INPUT closed). Posting history that requires correction after receipt review has been approved is not overwritten and may be reversed under the established authorization; stock dependencies apply §7.5 D1 and §10.2 D2, and existing authorizations/after-sales associations are retained.

### 7.2 Product Dates, Near-expiry and Batches (Withdrawn)

Withdrawn: product dates, near-expiry/expiry, batches and expired-sale blocking are completely deleted from P1 and P2; no extension points are retained. The numbering serves only historical tracking and is not a functional or acceptance requirement. Old rules and the original priority remain only in the v0.4 backup. Receipt, replenishment, sales, returns/exchanges and loss do not enter or select product dates/batches, and stock is aggregated by product and position; there are no related primary keys, foreign keys or precondition validations. Business operation time, promotion validity periods and the 30-day after-sales period are not within the scope of this withdrawal.

### 7.3 Promotion and Monetary Rules

Keep the existing pre-discount line-amount rules: unit price × quantity retains a 4-decimal intermediate value; line amounts use 2-decimal HALF_UP. Order totals and candidate comparisons use integer fen. Calculate each single promotion candidate separately, select the lowest order payable amount, and resolve equal payables using the existing explicit priority and promotion ID rules. Promotions do not stack.

Promotion validity, the 1–99% discount range, threshold-reduction boundaries and zero-price gift rules remain unchanged. A threshold is checked only against eligible lines' pre-discount total. A total of 0 cannot trigger a positive threshold, and division by zero is not performed.

After selecting an order discount of D fen, allocate it only to eligible sale lines. Let w be a line's pre-discount subtotal in integer fen and W the sum of eligible weights. If D>0, W must be >0. First allocate floor(D×w/W) to each line; distribute remaining fen by descending exact integer remainder, breaking equal remainders by immutable SALE_LINE.line_id ascending. Ineligible lines receive 0 discount. No generic multi-round capping/recalculation, renormalization or fallback equal-split algorithm is used.

Before confirmation, validate 0≤line discount≤eligible pre-discount amount, actual paid=original amount−discount≥0, Σline discounts=D and Σline actual paid=order payable. If any invariant fails, reject the entire calculation and create no partial sale record. Save product name, unit price, promotion and applicability, pre-discount amount, allocated discount, actual paid and stable sale-line ID. This supersedes the former final-line promotion-rounding rule; after-sales HALF_UP remains unchanged. Authority/history: §17 CR07-02.

### 7.4 Returns, Refund Allocation and Exchanges

Maintain an independent after-sales redemption ledger per original sale line. Q is purchased quantity and P its saved actual paid amount; valid returned quantity/amount are Qr/Mr, and exchanged quantity/original-item credit are Qx/Mx. Remaining quantity=Q−Qr−Qx; remaining amount=P−Mr−Mx. Both 0≤Qr+Qx≤Q and 0≤Mr+Mx≤P must hold. Exchanges consume original quantity and paid-amount quotas first; later returns use only the remainder. Later exchanges do not overwrite effective historical redemptions. Partial return/exchange quantity q must be positive and ≤remaining quantity. A non-final redemption uses min(HALF_UP(P×q÷Q,2),remaining amount); the final settlement of remaining quantity consumes all remaining amount. Refunds use the original line's saved actual paid amount, without cross-line reallocation or repricing using original/current prices or promotions. Exchange redemption consumes original-item credit, not replacement price or the actual cash difference. Same-product, same-quantity quality exchanges with zero cash difference still consume quantity and corresponding paid-amount quotas. Each redemption records original sale line, source after-sales document/line, quantity, amount, kind, operator, time, idempotency key and status. Duplicate requests consume no further quota. Refund, redemption and replacement on-shelf deduction are atomic and duplicate-safe, with no partial effect on failure. Authorized reversal releases original quantity/amount through linked reverse records, retaining originals and reasons, without physical deletion or repeated release.

The existing 30-day local-calendar limit, clerks' independent daily handling, and opened/quality eligibility remain effective. There is no manager exception to the time limit or eligibility. Select the reason as quality/other; other requires reason text but does not change eligibility. Replacement current price−this redemption's original-item credit is the exchange cash difference: positive means collect, negative means refund, zero means no difference. Same-product/same-quantity quality exchange has zero difference but still consumes both quotas. Replacement items are deducted only from on-shelf stock. All customer-returned goods stay outside inventory and are recorded as loss/disposition without deducting existing inventory again.



Independent business rule CR07-04: within one after-sales submission, calculate all exchange redemptions before refunds regardless of UI entry order; do not recalculate historical confirmed consumption. Store opened, quality_problem and reason_text per REDEMPTION rather than the header. A document may mix eligibility facts and exchange kinds. The header no longer carries a manager eligibility exception/exception reason. Other reasons require reason_text, which does not replace each line's eligibility facts.

Calculate per original sale line using this section's P×q/Q, HALF_UP and remaining-quota rules. Within the current submission, process split lines of the same kind by stable redemption_id ascending; do not use listed, procurement or current price. Only if the current submission exactly settles all quantity then remaining on that original line does the last settling detail in this order take the remaining fen. Non-final details use min(HALF_UP(P×q/Q,2),remaining amount at that point). Future after-sales operations must not rewrite amounts already confirmed.

exchange_kind belongs to REPLACEMENT. A special same-product, same-quantity quality exchange with zero difference explicitly references its same-document exchange CONSUME redemption through REPLACEMENT.quality_redemption_id. This unique reference requires quality_problem=true, identical original/replacement barcodes and quantities, and a 0 cash difference. Ordinary replacement lines leave that reference empty. Replacements deduct only on-shelf stock; returned goods do not re-enter inventory. Keep redeemed original paid credit, replacement amount and cash difference distinct. Special pairing must not waive the difference for other ordinary exchanges. See ERD §5 and CR07-01/03/04.

### 7.5 D1 Inventory Reversal Dependency Scope

Basis: CR07-06; approved status is found only in §17.1. Only **valid negative ledger entries for the same product and the same stock location whose commit_ordinal is greater than that of the original business ledger entry being reversed** are judged to be subsequent stock dependencies. Location W=in-warehouse, S=on-shelf. Ledger entries within the same commit sequence number are not subsequent dependencies of each other; time, ID, document version, and file ordering do not substitute for the commit sequence number. In the absence of batch conditions, block conservatively; do not infer which receipt was actually consumed, and do not use the product's total balance/net balance in place of the dependency check.

The "valid negative ledger entry" in this section means a negative ledger entry that has not been fully and authoritatively reversed; where the original entry and the reverse entry are paired item by item with a net amount of 0, it is deemed fully reversed and does not constitute a dependency.

| Subsequent action on the old receipt W+q | Dependency judgment |
|---|---|
| New receipt of the same code W+q | Does not block by itself, and does not clear another valid negative dependency |
| Replenishment W−q/S+q, in-warehouse loss registration W−q | A valid negative leg submitted later at location W for the same product blocks directly |
| Sale S−q, exchange-out S−q, on-shelf loss registration S−q | Does not directly block the W receipt; if a valid replenishment W− exists earlier, that replenishment blocks |
| Customer return / exchanged-back item | Not returned to stock, no stock ledger entries, does not constitute a stock dependency; the original sale's after-sales association constraints remain |
| Ledger entries within the same commit_ordinal | The same atomic business, does not constitute a "later-submitted" dependency |

Other stock sources/locations likewise check, per product and location, for later-submitted valid negative ledger entries, and the check is not extended to all events with the same code. A subsequent business can be fully excluded only when it is an authorized whole-document reversal, the original ledger entries and reverse ledger entries match pair by pair by product/location/quantity with a net amount of 0, and the business itself has no un-reversed after-sales/exchange/partial return; exclude the entire group consisting of the original ledger entries and their paired reverse entries, not just the reverse ones. A partial return, a partial exchange, or a refund only do not constitute a complete reversal of the original sale.

The object of a reversal is always the entire original business document. All products and locations of the original document are checked line by line; if the dependency of any line is not released, the entire document is rejected, no line's stock/status/payment/redemption takes partial effect, and no partial reverse ledger entries are generated. That other products do not affect the judgment refers only to products outside the original document. Non-negative stock after reversal is an additional safety check; existing authorization, original-sale after-sales association, idempotency, and audit constraints continue to apply.

### 10.2 D2 Monotonic Commit Ordinals within a Snapshot

Basis: CR07-07, forming a set with D1; for approval status see §17.1 only. P1 continues with collection/UTF-8 JSON single snapshot, temporary file + atomic replacement + previous-version backup; there is no database, COMMIT business entity, independent counter file or sorting module, and no commit_index is added without a requirement.

| Data | Logical type/required | Constraint/purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Snapshot root last_commit_ordinal | Integer; required | Non-negative, 0 for the initial empty snapshot; storage metadata, not a business entity; includes successful commits without stock ledger entries |
| STOCK_ENTRY.commit_ordinal | Integer; required | Positive integer, ≤ that snapshot's last_commit_ordinal; all stock ledger entries of the same successful commit have the same value, with no single-entry UK; different successful commits have increasing ordinals; used for D1 ordering judgment |

At startup, acquire a local lock file or OS file write lock under DATA_ROOT; if locking fails, be read-only or reject write operations, and prohibit a second process from writing concurrently. At the start of each business commit, read last under the single-writer lock protection; the candidate value last+1 exists only in that in-memory working copy. Place the candidate last, the business records and all ledger entries in **the same new JSON snapshot**; they take effect externally and the in-memory result is published only after the atomic replacement succeeds. A successful business commit without stock ledger entries also advances last; counting must not be based only on the number of stock events.

If any step of validation, serialization, temporary-file writing or atomic replacement fails, the persisted last is unchanged, the candidate does not take effect, records and ledger entries are invisible, and the old snapshot remains complete and startable; a retry may reuse the uncommitted candidate. A temporary file is not committed data and is not loaded as a business snapshot. If the replacement succeeds but a crash occurs before the response, the restart takes the new snapshot as authoritative, and the existing request idempotency key ensures that a retry does not double-post entries.

At startup/restore, read last and scan the commit_ordinal of all ledger entries; when there are no ledger entries, the maximum is treated as 0. If last<max or the type/positive-value constraints do not hold, the snapshot is inconsistent: block writes and prompt for restore/manual verification; last≥max is valid, and a greater value may arise from business without stock ledger entries. The next one starts from last+1. Restore only a complete snapshot and its associated attachments; counters/ledger entries/attachments from another source must not be mixed in; after restore, redo the integrity checks of associations, amounts, stock, dual quota, attachment existence/reference/hash and ordinals, and block writes with a prompt when anything is missing or inconsistent. Do not use created_at, ledger entry ID, document version or file ordering to prove commit order.

See AC-85; Ready only means the specification has an expectation, and actual results must be recorded after implementation is approved. DATA_ROOT has been decided to be the repository root data/; the backup sub-layout, root resolution and general attachment error semantics still reference §10.1, and D2 is not expanded into an undetermined technical process.

The read trace and the cancellation are each committed atomically and independently via IF-05; a successfully saved trace alone does not mean the after-sales is complete. For an unknown result, query along the original requestKey/original request; a failure is not treated as not committed; after the trace is committed, the subsequent after-sales must be reopened and revalidated.
